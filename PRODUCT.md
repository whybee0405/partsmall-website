# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Four overlapping trade personas, each with a different job on the site:

1. **Independent mechanic or workshop owner.** Time-pressured, car on the lift.
   Needs a phone number in under three taps. The branch finder is built for this
   person, which is why it sits high on the homepage and why WhatsApp is a
   first-class action.
2. **Fleet or procurement manager.** Volume buyer, wants a reliable account and
   proof the supplier will still exist in three years. Served by the network
   figures, the certifications and the trade-account flow.
3. **Car owner sourcing a specific part.** Price-conscious, part-number led.
   Served by the catalogue search and routed to a branch.
4. **Prospective distributor or agent.** Evaluating whether the network is real.
   Served by the map, the group story and the distributor path on `/wholesale`.

Context: mobile-heavy browsing across South Africa and neighbouring countries,
often on patchy connections and cheap Android hardware, frequently outdoors in
bright light. This is not a decorative detail, it decided the theme.

## Product purpose

Parts-Mall Africa is the South African head office and branch network site for
Parts-Mall Corporation, described by the Korean parent as its "sales subsidiary
and hub for sales and distribution across the African continent."

33 branches across 9 South African provinces plus 5 pan-African country points,
38 network points in total.

This is a wholesale and distribution site, not a retail webshop. No cart, no
checkout, no published pricing. Success is a workshop, fleet buyer or distributor
trusting the network enough to call a branch or start a trade conversation.

Note: Parts-Mall Group also operates a separate consumer retail arm (PartZone).
This site is specifically the distribution hub, consistent with the parent's own
positioning.

## Brand personality

Confident, industrial, trustworthy. A supplier that has done this at scale for
years and knows its network cold. Direct, mechanical, dependable.

Not Seoul-exported. Not a generic auto-parts stock-photo brand. Grounded in real
branch data, real provinces, real private-brand names.

## Anti-references

- Floating gradient orbs, centred-stat heroes, glassmorphism, the generic "why
  choose us" three-icon row, exploded-engine-diagram decoration, chrome and
  carbon-fibre textures, wrench-icon soup, lorem ipsum
- **Condensed display type.** The entire automotive parts category uses it. It is
  the first-order reflex and this brand goes wide instead
- **Navy-plus-safety-orange or racing red.** The motorsport palette reflex. This
  is a distribution business, not a performance brand
- The repeated eyebrow, heading, lead-paragraph grammar that makes every section
  of a B2B site look identical
- Identical "two white cards side by side" B2B template layouts
- Stat walls with no texture or narrative

## Design principles

1. **Expertise and scale earn the space.** Real branch counts, real private-brand
   names, real provinces. Never an invented statistic. Counts are derived in code
   so they cannot drift out of date.
2. **South African, not Seoul-exported.** Grounded, practical, industrial-trade.
3. **The network is the proof.** Branch data, corporate figures and warranty
   backing are the brand statement, not lifestyle photography.
4. **Speed is the product.** The mechanic with a car on the lift is the hardest
   user to serve and the one who decides whether the site works. Call, WhatsApp
   and directions are always visible, never behind a hover or a detail page.
5. **Wholesale first, retail-adjacent.** CTAs are enquiry and branch-contact,
   never buy-now.

## Voice

Plain trade English. Short sentences. Say the thing.

Copy is written for someone standing at a car, not for a search engine. "We call
before we email" beats "our team will endeavour to make contact." The guides are
the clearest expression of this and set the register for the rest of the site.

No em dashes. No filler verbs (elevate, seamless, unleash, next-gen). No
poetic-craftsman section labels.

## Accessibility

WCAG 2.1 AA minimum. Skip link, visible focus rings, aria-labels on icon-only
controls, semantic headings, no horizontal scroll at 360px, availability states
that carry a word as well as a colour, reduced-motion respected globally.
